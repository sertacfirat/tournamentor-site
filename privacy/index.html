Privacy policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026. App: Tournamentor (Android), published by Sertaç Fırat. Contact: sertacfirat0@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no network access. We, the developer, never receive any information from or about you.

Where your data lives

Players, tournaments and results you enter are stored only on your device. If Android backup is turned on, Android may copy this data to your own Google account as part of its automatic, encrypted device backup, so it can be restored on a new phone. The developer cannot read these backups. You can turn backup off in Android settings, and uninstalling the app deletes its data from the device.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data. If you choose to share something with Android's share sheet, it goes only where you send it.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. On Google Play it is listed for ages 13 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect in the app.

Geliştirici bu yedekleri okuyamaz. Yedeklemeyi Android ayarlarından kapatabilirsin; uygulamayı kaldırmak verilerini cihazdan siler.
